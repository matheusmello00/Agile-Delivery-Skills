# Retrospective formats and mechanisms

Claude knows these formats; what this reference fixes is **the selection criterion** and the mechanisms that help when the conversation is difficult. Always explain why you suggest this format and not another.

## Quick choice

| Team's moment | Format | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Team new to retros; wants something actionable | Start / Stop / Continue | quick, produces actions; risk of staying superficial if it becomes routine |
| Emotionally charged sprint | Mad / Sad / Glad | gives room to "how did you feel"; reserve time for actions or it stays a vent |
| Learning sprint, going beyond good/bad | 4 Ls | rich in insight; needs more time and facilitation |
| Forces holding the team back that nobody names | Sailboat | good for impediments and risks; the metaphor distracts very pragmatic teams |
| Problem recurring sprint after sprint (chronic carryover, scope creep) | Deep-dive on one theme | only works if the team accepts spending the whole session on it |
| Continuous flow with WIP and queues | Flow health / WIP / Queue review | good for analyzing wait time, lead time, columns and pulls that were blocked |
| Kanban with a recurring bottleneck | Bottleneck / Wait / Pull review | focuses on what holds back throughput and where the item goes into waiting |

## Collection mechanisms

Orthogonal to the format — choose the mechanism too, not just the format.

| Mechanism | When | What it solves |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous collection | low morale, fear of reprisals, one topic or person dominates | it is what makes those sprints/flows discussable; without it the format is not enough |
| Prior asynchronous collection (24–48h before) | distributed team, partners in other time zones | the synchronous session focuses on insights and actions, not on collecting |
| Silent writing (write before discussing) | large teams, a few voices dominate | reduces anchoring and the first-opinion effect |
| Wait / flow signals collection | Kanban with an incoming backlog in queues and excess WIP | makes visible the moments of waiting and pulling that need a decision |

## Facilitation note

Keep the five phases (set the stage, gather data, generate insights, decide what to do, close): the format changes the gather phase, the mechanism changes **how** it is gathered. Default agenda 60 min — 5 / 15 / 20 / 15 / 5. Above ~8 people, gather in breakouts and merge in plenary. Reserve real time to decide actions: a retro without actions is a pleasant conversation that changes nothing. In Kanban, the focus of the retro is how the board and the flow are producing/pulling value and where production was interrupted.
